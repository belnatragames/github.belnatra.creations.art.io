Custom Commissions

I offer custom artwork and handmade jewelry commissions. Whether you're looking for a personalized illustration, a custom sticker design, or a unique jewelry piece, I’d love to collaborate with you.

What I Currently Offer

  • Custom character illustrations
  • Personalized sticker designs
  • Custom charm jewelry (color/theme-based)
  • Small batch handmade gift items

How It Works

  1. Submit an inquiry through the Contact page.
  2. We discuss details, timeline, and pricing.
  3. I create a sketch or concept for approval.
  4. Final piece is completed and delivered/shipped.

Pricing & Timeline

Pricing varies depending on complexity, size, and materials. Most small illustration commissions start at $25. Jewelry commissions vary by materials used.

Turnaround time typically ranges from 1–3 weeks depending on current workload.
